FAQ

What to know before you start

Do I need a cloud account?

No. The personal edition runs locally with SQLite. Whether AI inference uses local or remote endpoints depends on your explicit configuration.

Can I use AIKS without an AI model?

Yes. You can browse imported conversations and use textual search. Automatically extracting new knowledge requires an enabled and configured AI endpoint.

Will AIKS modify my coding-agent history?

No. Local providers read only allowed source files. SiYuan sync checks for changes and does not silently overwrite user-edited documents.

Does it fetch every private ChatGPT conversation?

No. Web-source imports support authorized public share links, not full access to private logged-in account histories.
